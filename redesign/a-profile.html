Energy economist working on power markets, market design, and the economics of the energy transition.

I combine industrial organization, hands-on regulatory experience, and data science to study how energy markets are structured, priced, and governed. At Rice University's Baker Institute I work on electricity retail competition, green hydrogen, and critical minerals. Before that I spent four years with the World Bank's Markets & Competition team and led the economic research unit of Peru's competition authority.

Focus areas

Electricity markets & pricing

Retail competition, price dispersion, consumer switching, and market power, with a focus on ERCOT.

Energy transition

Green hydrogen hubs, critical minerals and trade, clean-power targets, and climate finance.

Competition & regulation

Antitrust, product-market regulation, and competitive neutrality across 10+ economies.

Data science & econometrics

Python, R, and Stata; ML and LLM tooling; graduate teaching in statistics for data science.
