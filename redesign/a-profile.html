I am an economist at Rice University. I work on competition and pricing in energy markets, especially retail electricity, and on the economics of the energy transition: hydrogen, critical minerals, and clean-power policy.

At the Baker Institute's Center for Energy Studies I am a Fellow in Energy and Market Design, and I teach in Rice's MS in Energy Economics and Online MS in Data Science. Before Rice, I spent four years as an economist with the World Bank's Markets and Competition team, and earlier led the economic research unit at INDECOPI, Peru's competition authority. I received my PhD in economics from Stony Brook University.

Areas of expertise

  • Electricity markets: retail competition, price dispersion, consumer switching, and market power.
  • Energy transition: green hydrogen, critical minerals and trade, clean-power targets, and climate finance.
  • Competition policy and regulation: antitrust, product-market regulation, and competitive neutrality.
  • Data analysis: econometrics and machine learning in Python, R, and Stata.
