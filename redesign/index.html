Website redesign, three options

All three use the same content. They differ in layout and typography.

  1. A. Profile: photo and contact in a fixed left column, one scrolling column of text on the right.
  2. B. Résumé: name and one-line pitch across the top, then labeled rows like a typeset résumé. Aimed at industry readers.
  3. C. Research: a narrow serif page in the style of an economist's personal site, with a publication list that can be filtered by topic.
