Energy economist

I study how energy markets are structured, priced, and governed, from Texas electricity retail to the minerals behind the energy transition.

Fellow in Energy and Market Design, Center for Energy Studies, Rice University's Baker Institute · Lecturer in Economics, Rice University

tilsa@rice.edu · CV · LinkedIn · Houston, TX

Themes

Electricity markets. Retail competition, price dispersion, and consumer switching. My training in industrial organization started with switching costs in telecommunications and now applies the same tools to ERCOT.

The energy transition. The economics and governance of green hydrogen hubs, critical-mineral supply chains and trade, clean-power targets, and climate finance in developing economies.

Competition and regulation. Four years with the World Bank's Markets & Competition team assessing product-market regulation and competitive neutrality, and earlier leading economic research at Peru's competition authority.

Data. Applied econometrics and machine learning in Python, R, and Stata. I teach graduate statistics in Rice's Online MS in Data Science.
