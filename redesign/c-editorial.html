Tilsa Oré Mónago

I am an economist working on how energy markets are organized and regulated. My current research studies price dispersion and competition in retail electricity, the trade and governance of critical minerals, and the conditions for competitive green hydrogen hubs.

I am a Fellow in Energy and Market Design at the Center for Energy Studies of Rice University's Baker Institute for Public Policy, and a Lecturer in Economics at Rice. Earlier I worked on competition policy at the World Bank and at INDECOPI, Peru's competition authority. My PhD is from Stony Brook University.

tilsa@rice.edu, Baker Hall 206, Houston. LinkedIn.

Areas of expertise

Electricity markets, in particular retail competition, consumer switching, and market power. The energy transition: hydrogen, critical minerals and trade, clean-power targets, and climate finance. Competition policy and economic regulation, from antitrust to state ownership and competitive neutrality. Applied econometrics and machine learning, which I teach at the graduate level.
